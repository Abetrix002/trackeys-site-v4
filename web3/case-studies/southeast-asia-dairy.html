Challenge

Sales per rep was plateauing. Reps followed fixed beat plans and missed upsell opportunities, especially on high-margin premium SKUs.

Solution

AI sales copilot surfaced upsell cues per customer based on order history and SKU affinity. Route optimization cut drive time and added 2 visits per day.
