HOW IT WORKS

From forecast to fulfilled.

How an order moves through Trackeys DMS when every node is connected.

  1. STEP 01

    Forecast

    AI predicts next-period demand per SKU per distributor per week, with promotional lift modeled in.

  2. STEP 02

    Reorder

    Automated triggers generate replenishment orders against the forecast and safety stock.

  3. STEP 03

    Allocate

    Available stock allocates across distributors based on forecast, performance and priority.

  4. STEP 04

    Fulfill

    Warehouses pick, pack and ship. Everything tracked at batch / lot level.

  5. STEP 05

    Deliver

    Handoff to the Delivery module, the same system that ships to distributors or direct to trade.

  6. STEP 06

    Measure

    Fulfillment rate, accuracy, lead time and distributor scorecards roll up automatically.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Trackeys handle multi-tier distribution?

Fully. Plant → regional DC → sub-distributor → retail is modeled as a first-class network. Each tier has its own visibility, pricing, credit and reporting, with roll-up all the way back to the brand owner.

What forecasting models are used?

An ensemble: classical time-series (SARIMA, Prophet), gradient boosting (LightGBM) on rich feature sets, and neural approaches for long-horizon and promotional lift. The system picks the best model per SKU per distributor based on cross-validation.

Can distributors use their own mobile apps?

Yes. Distributors use the white-labeled Trackeys distributor app or web portal. Orders, stock checks, payments and performance reports are all self-service.

Does it handle expiry, batch and lot tracking?

Yes, full FEFO/FIFO logic at warehouse and distributor level. Expiry alerts fire ahead of time so product can be rotated, discounted or returned before write-off.
