HOW IT WORKS

How a shelf becomes a decision.

From a merchandiser's phone camera to a category manager's dashboard, in minutes.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How accurate is the vision AI?

SKU recognition accuracy averages 99%+ for well-lit shelf photos of products in the trained catalog. New SKUs can be added via few-shot learning, typically 10–20 reference images per SKU get the model to production accuracy.

Does it work in low light or on messy shelves?

Yes, the model is trained on a large dataset of real-world retail photos including poor lighting, partial occlusion and disordered shelves. For extreme conditions the app prompts the merchandiser to reshoot with guidance.

Can I define custom planograms?

Absolutely. Planograms are uploaded per store + category, with flexibility for retailer-specific layouts. The AI scores against whichever planogram applies at that specific store on that specific visit.

What about competitor data collection?

Merchandisers can capture competitor prices, facings and promos in the same flow. The data flows into competitive dashboards, useful for pricing teams and brand managers.
