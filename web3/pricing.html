FAQ

Pricing, modules, contracts, clearly.

How does per-module pricing work?

Each tier bundles a set of modules (SFA, Delivery & Routing, Merchandising AI, DMS). If you only need SFA + Delivery, pick Starter. If you need all four, pick Growth. Enterprise unlocks everything plus custom modules and workflows. You can also add Fleet Management as a separate add-on on any tier.

What counts as a "visit" or a "shelf scan"?

A visit is a single rep check-in at a store or customer location. A shelf scan is one photo processed by the vision AI (a single aisle typically takes 2–4 scans). Both limits are monthly and reset on your billing anniversary.

Can we change tiers or add modules mid-contract?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at renewal. Adding an add-on (Fleet, Premium Support, etc.) is instant with pro-rated billing to your anniversary.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, 14 days on Starter or Growth, no credit card required. You get full access to the modules in that tier and we'll help you import sample data so you can evaluate with your own customers and stores.

What's included in onboarding?

Starter is self-serve with guided setup videos. Growth includes a 2-week guided onboarding with a specialist (data migration, user training, integration setup). Enterprise gets a white-glove program: 6–12 weeks with a dedicated success manager, custom workflow design and change-management support.

Is there a discount for nonprofits or education?

Yes, 40% off Growth for registered nonprofits and 25% off for accredited education institutions. Contact us with documentation to apply.

How is data security handled?

SOC 2 Type II certified. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Enterprise plans include SSO, SCIM provisioning, advanced audit logs, data residency options (US, EU, UAE) and optional on-prem deployment.

What happens if we exceed visit or scan limits?

You're never blocked. Overages are billed at $0.05 per extra visit and $0.08 per extra shelf scan. We alert you when you hit 80% of your limit so you can upgrade or manage usage proactively.
