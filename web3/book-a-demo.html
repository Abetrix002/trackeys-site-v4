FAQ

What to expect from the demo.

How long is the demo?

30 minutes for the standard demo. We reserve an optional 15-minute buffer for Q&A.

Who should join from our side?

Ideally 2–3 people: the operational owner (sales/ops/distribution), a tech stakeholder, and a decision-maker. We adapt content to the attendees.

Can we see it on our own data?

Yes. Send a sample (CSV, Excel, or a read-only export) 24 hours ahead and we'll load it into a sandbox before the call.

Is there a commitment?

None. No credit card, no NDA required for the demo. If you want to pilot, we sign a lightweight mutual NDA and a pilot-scope doc.
