FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Trackeys handle both modern trade and traditional trade?

Yes. Modern trade (hypers, supers, convenience chains) gets full planogram compliance, contract SKU tracking and promotional execution. Traditional trade gets van sales, cash collection, credit management and beat-plan coverage, all from the same app with role-based configuration.

Can we manage trade schemes and promo mechanics?

Yes. Complex trade schemes (buy X get Y, tiered discounts, display support, secondary placement) are configured at HQ, enforced at order capture and reconciled against secondary sales automatically.

How is secondary sales tracked?

Distributors log secondary sales through the DMS portal or mobile app. Van sales direct to trade is captured at source. Both feed into one secondary-sales view with full SKU-level traceability.

Does it support multi-country operations?

Yes. Multi-entity, multi-currency, multi-tax and multi-language, with per-country configuration for trade schemes, pricing and compliance rules.
