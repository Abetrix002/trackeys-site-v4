FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can we connect our existing telematics?

Yes, native connectors for Samsara, Geotab, Motive and others. For proprietary telematics, a standardized API ingests vehicle, driver and diagnostic data into the Trackeys live view.

Does it handle cold chain and temperature-sensitive shipments?

Yes. IoT temperature and humidity sensors stream continuously. Breach alerts fire in real time and full logs attach to every shipment for compliance, claims and dispute handling.

How does the AI handle dynamic re-routing?

When traffic, cancellations or priority changes occur, the engine re-evaluates remaining stops in seconds against time-window, vehicle and driver constraints, and pushes the updated manifest to the driver automatically.

Can our customers track their own shipments?

Yes. White-labeled tracking pages and real-time SMS/email updates give end customers self-service visibility without exposing internal operations.
