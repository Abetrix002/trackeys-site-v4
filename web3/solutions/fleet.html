HOW IT WORKS

From key turn to scheduled service.

How a vehicle-day runs through Trackeys.

  1. STEP 01

    Assign

    Vehicle and driver allocated to job based on fit, hours and availability.

  2. STEP 02

    Inspect

    Pre-trip DVIR captures vehicle condition with photos and sign-off.

  3. STEP 03

    Track

    Location, speed, and safety events stream live to ops.

  4. STEP 04

    Deliver

    Jobs completed with POD. Mileage and fuel captured automatically.

  5. STEP 05

    Close

    Post-trip DVIR, driver settlement and vehicle parking logged.

  6. STEP 06

    Maintain

    Upcoming PM and alerts surface; workshop slots auto-scheduled.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Trackeys work with our existing GPS hardware?

Yes. Certified connectors for Samsara, Geotab, Motive and most major telematics providers. For proprietary or niche devices, a standardized ingest API handles the rest.

How is driver behavior scored?

A composite score weighs speeding (vs. posted limits), harsh braking, acceleration, cornering, idling and seatbelt compliance. Weights are configurable per fleet profile and the engine learns from your historical data.

Can drivers see their own scorecards?

Yes. Drivers get a mobile scorecard with their own trends, coaching tips and peer comparisons. Gamification is optional, with leaderboards that reward safety and efficiency.

Does it handle mixed fleets (trucks, vans, cars, bikes)?

Yes. Vehicle profiles model weight, volume, fuel type, emissions and job fit. The routing and allocation engine respects constraints for every vehicle class.
